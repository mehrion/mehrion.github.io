Email Us

Response time

We aim to reply within 3 business days. Data deletion and privacy requests are handled within 30 days.

What to include

To help us resolve things quickly, please include:

  • The name and version of the app (shown in Android’s App info for the app).
  • Your Android version and your device model.
  • What you expected to happen, what actually happened, and steps to reproduce it.
  • A screenshot if possible. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Can I set up an Ivory app for a parent or grandparent?

Yes. Ivory apps have only a few settings, chosen once, so a family member can set one up for a parent, grandparent or elderly relative in a moment.

How do I delete my data?

Ivory apps have no accounts and keep nothing on a server. Everything is stored on your phone and is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. See our Account & Data Deletion page for the steps.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Google Play, so request a refund through Google Play’s refund process, or email us and we will help where we can.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Ivory apps have no subscriptions. Each app is a one-time purchase.

What data do your apps collect?

Ivory apps collect no data and have no internet permission. Read our Privacy Policy; each app’s store listing also includes a data safety section.

I want to report abuse or unsafe content.

Email us with details and, where possible, a screenshot. See our Child Safety page for how we handle reports.
