Email Us

Response time

We aim to reply within 3 business days. Data deletion and privacy requests are handled within 30 days.

What to include

To help us resolve things quickly, please include:

  • The name and version of the app (usually shown on the app’s settings or About screen).
  • Your operating system and version (for example Android, iOS, Windows, macOS or Linux) and your device model.
  • What you expected to happen, what actually happened, and steps to reproduce it.
  • A screenshot if possible. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account or data?

See our Account & Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by the store or platform you bought from (for example Google Play, the Apple App Store or the Microsoft Store). Request a refund through that store’s refund process, or email us and we will help where we can.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you subscribed through. Open that store’s subscription settings (for example, in Google Play: profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), select the subscription, then choose Cancel.

What data do your apps collect?

Read our Privacy Policy. Each app’s store listing may also include a data safety or privacy section.

I want to report abuse or unsafe content.

Email us with details and, where possible, a screenshot. See our Child Safety page for how we handle reports.
