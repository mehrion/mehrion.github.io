1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the apps and software published by mehrion (“mehrion”, “we”, “us”), on any platform, and to this website, mehrion.github.io. It explains what information is handled, why, and what choices you have.

Where an app is distributed through a store such as Google Play, the Apple App Store or the Microsoft Store, that store’s listing may include a data safety or privacy section describing the data that specific app collects or shares. If an app’s own listing or in-app notice is more specific than this policy, that more specific description applies to that app.

2. Ivory apps

Ivory apps collect no data. No Ivory app has the internet permission, an account, ads, analytics or an advertising ID.

Ivory is the family of simple Android apps published by mehrion, starting with Ivory Calculator. For every Ivory app:

  • No internet. No Ivory app declares the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything to mehrion or to anyone else. This is checked on the merged manifest of every release.
  • No collection or sharing. mehrion does not collect, receive, share or sell any data from Ivory apps. The data safety section of each app’s store listing says the same.
  • No accounts, ads or tracking. There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no advertising ID.
  • Data stays on your phone. The few things an app remembers, such as its settings or, in Ivory Calculator, your last answer and your past sums, are stored only on your device, and are removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it.
  • Permissions. Ivory Calculator asks for no permissions at all. If a later Ivory app needs a permission for its one job, it says so on its page and in its store listing, asks only when you use that feature, and still never connects to the internet.

The sections below describe the general practices that apply to mehrion apps and this website. Where they mention optional collection, accounts, analytics or ads, none of that applies to Ivory apps.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain our apps and the features you ask for.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure, where an app has accounts.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs and improve performance and usability.
  • To process purchases and subscriptions (payments are handled by the store or platform you buy from).
  • To show advertising, where an app is ad-supported.
  • To respond to your support requests and to comply with legal obligations.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the app), legitimate interests (security, diagnostics, improvement), consent (for example, personalised ads or optional permissions) and legal obligation.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run our apps, and only as needed:

  • App stores and platform services (for example Google Play, the Apple App Store and the Microsoft Store) for distribution, purchases and subscriptions. These providers have their own privacy policies.
  • Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics, where an app uses them, for analytics and crash reporting. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google AdMob, where an app shows ads. See AdMob data usage. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device’s advertising settings.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights, safety and security, or as part of a business transfer, in which case we will tell you of any material change in handling of your data.

Which of these services a particular app uses is stated in that app’s store listing or in-app privacy notice.

6. Device permissions

Apps may request device permissions (for example, notifications, camera, photos, files or network access) only when needed for a feature you use. Where your operating system allows, permissions are requested at the time of use, and you can change or revoke them at any time in your device’s settings. Declining a permission may disable the related feature.

7. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above. Account data is kept while your account is active and is deleted after you request deletion (see Account & Data Deletion), except where we must keep limited records to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent fraud. Diagnostic data is kept for a limited period determined by the relevant provider. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal information, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

  • Delete your data: follow the steps on our Account & Data Deletion page.
  • Other requests: contact us through our support page (use the Email Us button). We may need to verify your identity and will respond within 30 days.
  • Ads: reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

We do not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

9. Children’s privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) unless an app’s store listing says it is designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. See also our Child Safety page.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, advertising or analytics trackers, and has no accounts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser information. The hosting provider (GitHub) may also process standard server logs; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
