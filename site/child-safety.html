Safety

Child safety standards.

Our commitment to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and protecting young people.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Our commitment

mehrion has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Our apps must never be used to create, share, store, solicit or promote content or behaviour that sexualises or endangers children.

What we prohibit

  • Any content depicting or promoting the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation or sexualised contact with minors.
  • Sharing or requesting images, videos or personal details of minors in a harmful way.
  • Using our apps to facilitate trafficking or any other form of child exploitation.

Accounts and content that violate these standards are removed and users are permanently banned where an app has such features.

How to report a concern

If you see content or behaviour in one of our apps that may endanger a child, tell us right away:

  1. Use the Email Us button on our support page with the subject Child safety report.
  2. Include the app name, the account or content involved, and any relevant details. Do not forward the illegal material itself.
  3. We review reports promptly, remove violating content, and take action on the accounts involved.

Cooperation with authorities

We comply with applicable child safety laws. Where we become aware of apparent CSAM, we will report it to the relevant authorities, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the appropriate national hotline, and cooperate with law enforcement.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Helpful resources

  • NCMEC CyberTipline (United States)
  • INHOPE hotlines (international)
  • Internet Watch Foundation (United Kingdom)

Designated contact

Our child safety point of contact is reachable through our support page. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
