Your data

Account & data deletion.

Ivory apps have no accounts and keep nothing on a server. Here is how to clear what is stored on your phone, and how to request deletion from any mehrion app that has accounts.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Ivory apps: nothing to delete on a server

Ivory apps have no account and never connect to the internet. mehrion never receives your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

The few things an Ivory app remembers, such as its settings or its history, are stored only on your phone. To remove them:

  1. Inside the app, use its clear-history option, where it has one, to remove the history (you will be asked to confirm).
  2. Or open Android Settings, then Apps, choose the Ivory app, then Storage and Clear data.
  3. Or uninstall the app, which removes everything it stored.

Other mehrion apps with accounts

If a mehrion app ever offers accounts, you can request deletion in either of these ways.

Option 1: Delete from the app

Where an app has accounts, you can delete your account from inside the app:

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (or Profile).
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, or the app has no in-app option, email us from the address linked to your account:

  1. Use the Email Us button on our support page from the address linked to your account, with the subject Account deletion request.
  2. Include the name of the app and the email or username of the account.
  3. We may reply to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password.
  4. We will confirm once the deletion is complete.

We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in details.
  • Content and settings you created and stored with us.
  • Data linked to your account in our databases and storage.

What may be kept

  • Limited records we are legally required to keep, such as purchase and tax records, or needed to resolve disputes and prevent fraud, retained only as long as required.
  • Anonymised or aggregated data that can no longer identify you.
  • Routine backups, which are overwritten on their normal cycle (generally within 30 to 90 days).

Data stored only on your device

Data that never leaves your device is removed when you clear the app’s data in your device settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Data held by stores and other providers

Purchases are managed by the store you bought from (for example Google Play). Data held by those providers (for example your store account) is controlled through your account settings with them, such as your Google Account.
