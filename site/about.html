About

About mehrion and Ivory.

An independent developer making beautifully simple phone apps: clean, quiet and private, with nothing you don’t need.

Who we are

mehrion is an independent app developer. We design, build and publish Ivory, a family of calm, simple Android apps, and we maintain this website as the home for our apps, support and policies.

What Ivory is

Ivory apps are the everyday tools on a phone, made simple. Each one does a single job, in a clean, uncluttered design with clear type and generous spacing. They are made for seniors and older adults, for the family members who set a phone up for a parent or grandparent, and for anyone who finds modern apps noisy and cluttered. See every app on the apps page.

How Ivory apps are optimised.

Every Ivory app is designed around the way eyesight, hearing, hands, memory and confidence change as we age, so it is comfortable for seniors and older adults and just as easy for everyone else. The same rules are built into every app, and automated tests check them on every screen of every release.

One typeface across the whole suite

Every Ivory app uses Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all of its text. Atkinson Hyperlegible, named after the founder of the Braille Institute, was developed specifically to increase legibility for readers with low vision, and to improve comprehension. Its letters and numbers are drawn so they cannot be mistaken for one another, including a slashed zero.

  • Built into every app, so the text always looks exactly right, with nothing to download.
  • Generously sized, comfortable text that grows with your phone’s text size setting.
  • Clear, confident letterforms only. Thin, faint text is never used.
  • Sentence case and steady, evenly spaced numbers, so nothing jumps around.
  • Used exactly as designed, and never swapped for another font.
  • Text follows your phone’s text size setting, even at the very largest, with nothing cut off.

What changes with age, and what Ivory does about it

What changesWhat it can mean on a phoneWhat Ivory does
EyesightSmall text is hard to read, bright white screens dazzle, and thin grey text disappears.A warm ivory background instead of glaring white, strong contrast, a typeface made for readability and comfortable text size.
HearingQuiet or high-pitched sounds are missed.Where an app makes a sound, it is low-pitched and clear, with vibration alongside. Alarms start quietly and grow louder, and nothing plays unexpectedly.
HandsTremor, arthritis and dry fingertips cause missed taps and accidental double taps.Generously sized, well-spaced buttons that need just a single tap, so a shaky or accidental touch never causes trouble.
MemorySteps and places are easy to forget, and menus are easy to get lost in.Short, simple journeys that never go more than two screens deep, the same layout in every app, plain titles on every screen and automatic saving.
Processing speedMore time is needed to read and decide.No time limits and no vanishing messages: confirmations stay on screen until the next action.
ConfidenceFear of breaking the phone, of costs, or of looking foolish.Risky actions ask first, with the safe choice on top, and can be undone. Where an app can keep a deleted item, it is kept for 30 days. The price is shown before you buy and paid once.
Scams and clutterOlder people are frequent targets of phone and message fraud.No ads, no pop-ups, no internet and no sign-in, so there is nothing to tap that leads away from the app.
Reading and languageText-heavy screens are a barrier, and not everyone reads English first.An icon and a word on every button, plain everyday language, an optional read-aloud in the phone’s own voice, and ready for other languages, including right-to-left ones.

Built the same way, every time

  • One design family. The same look, words, icons and button positions in every app, so learning one teaches all of them.
  • One calm, light look. A warm ivory background and a deep navy accent, quiet and uncluttered. Colour always comes with a word and an icon.
  • Steady screens. Nothing moves under your finger: boxes keep their size, messages appear in space that is already reserved, and buttons never change shape.
  • Everything visible. Every action is a labelled button with an icon and a word. No hidden menus, swipes or long presses.
  • Works with your phone’s accessibility tools. Screen reader, voice control and switch control, plus the largest text and display sizes.
  • Made for Android. Fast and light, and comfortable on older phones.
  • Carefully tested. Every screen is checked at normal and very large text sizes, so nothing is cut off, hard to read or hard to tap.

What is Ivory?

Which typeface do Ivory apps use?

Every Ivory app uses Atkinson Hyperlegible Next across the whole suite. Atkinson Hyperlegible, named after the founder of the Braille Institute, was developed specifically to increase legibility for readers with low vision, and to improve comprehension.

How are Ivory apps designed for older adults?

Ivory apps are built around how eyesight, hearing, hands, memory and confidence change with age.
