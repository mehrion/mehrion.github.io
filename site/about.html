About

About mehrion and Ivory.

An independent developer making beautifully simple phone apps: clean, quiet and private, with nothing you don’t need.

Who we are

mehrion is an independent app developer. We design, build and publish Ivory, a family of calm, simple Android apps, and we maintain this website as the home for our apps, support and policies.

What Ivory is

Ivory apps are the everyday tools on a phone, made simple: a calculator, notes, a checklist and more to come. Each one does a single job, in a clean, uncluttered design with clear type and generous spacing. They are made for seniors and older adults, for the family members who set a phone up for a parent or grandparent, and for anyone who finds modern apps noisy and cluttered. Our first app is Ivory Calculator; see all of them on the apps page.

Why Ivory.

Clean, quiet and private, with nothing you don’t need.

No internet needed

No Ivory app asks for the internet permission. They work fully offline, and nothing is ever sent to a server.

No ads, no subscriptions

Nothing flashing, nothing interrupting. Each app is a one-time purchase, with the price shown before you buy.

No account, no tracking

No sign-in, no analytics and no data selling. What you create stays on your phone.

One job, done well

Only the features that matter, and no AI features, in a clean, uncluttered design with clear type (Atkinson Hyperlegible) and generous spacing.

Easy to set up for family

Only a few settings, chosen once. A parent, grandparent or elderly relative can use an Ivory app the same day.

Small and light

A download of a few megabytes that starts quickly, so Ivory apps run well on older, hand-me-down phones.

What we believe

  • Be clear. Apps should say what they do and what data they use, in plain language.
  • Collect less. If an app does not need a piece of data, we do not ask for it.
  • Stay in control. Your data stays on your phone and goes when you uninstall the app.
  • Keep it simple. When in doubt, we leave it out.

Questions

What is Ivory?

Ivory is a family of calm, simple Android apps made by mehrion. Each app does one job well, in a clean, uncluttered design, with no ads, no account and no tracking.

Do Ivory apps need the internet?

No. No Ivory app asks for the internet permission, so they work fully offline and nothing is sent to a server.

Do Ivory apps have ads or subscriptions?

No. There are no ads and no subscriptions. Each app is a one-time purchase, shown before you buy.

Are Ivory apps suitable for seniors and older adults?

Yes. Ivory apps are designed with seniors and older adults in mind, and they are just as easy for everyone else. They use clear type, generous spacing and only a few settings, so a family member can set one up for a parent or grandparent in a moment.

Which Ivory apps are available?

Ivory Calculator is the first app and is being prepared for release on Google Play. More Ivory apps will follow, one at a time.

Who makes Ivory?

Ivory is made by mehrion, an independent developer. You can reach mehrion through the support page on this website.
