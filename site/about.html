About mehrion.

An independent developer making apps that are useful, honest and respectful of your data.

Who we are

mehrion is an independent app developer. We design, build and publish apps across platforms, and we maintain this website as the home for our apps, support and policies.

What we believe

  • Be clear. Apps should say what they do and what data they use, in plain language.
  • Collect less. If an app does not need a piece of data, we do not ask for it.
  • Stay in control. You should be able to export or delete your data without a fight.
  • Keep it simple. Fewer features, done well, beat a crowded screen.
