What it does.

One job, done well: simple sums for bills, shopping, change, discounts and budgets.

The whole sum stays in view

You see 1,250 + 380 as you type, not just the last number, so you can check it before you press equals. The answer sits above the keys and is always in view.

Shop-style percentages

Works like a till: 200 − 10% = 180, 200 + 15% = 230, 200 × 10% = 20. The display explains it, for example “10% of 200 = 20”.

Past sums

The last 20 calculations, like a till receipt. Tap one to use its answer again. Kept on your phone only, and clearable at any time.

Copy the result

One tap copies the answer so you can paste it into a message. The button turns green and says “Copied” so you know it worked.

Optional read aloud

Switch it on and a Speak button reads the answer out loud, for example “one thousand six hundred thirty”.

Exact decimals

0.1 + 0.2 is exactly 0.3. It uses exact decimal arithmetic, so everyday money sums come out right.

About this calculator.

Ivory Calculator is a calculator for everyday sums: checking a bill, adding up the shopping, working out change, a discount in a shop, a pension or a monthly budget. It does that one job and nothing else. There are no scientific functions, brackets, memory keys or currency conversion, because every extra button is one more thing to get wrong.

It is part of Ivory, a family of calm, simple, private Android apps made by mehrion. Like every Ivory app it has no ads, no account, no tracking and no internet permission, and it is built to run well on small, older phones.

Who it is for.

Ivory Calculator is designed with seniors and older adults in mind, and it is just as easy for everyone else. It also suits family members who set up a phone for a parent or grandparent: there are only two settings, so it is ready in a moment. If you are tired of calculators full of adverts and permissions, it is for you too.

  • Clear type with generous spacing, and text that follows your phone’s font size setting.
  • Large, well-spaced keys that keep the same size and place in every state.
  • The whole calculator on one screen at normal text size, with no hidden menus.
  • A light, calm look with strong contrast.

Frequently asked questions.

What is Ivory Calculator?

Ivory Calculator is a simple calculator app for Android phones and tablets, made by mehrion. It is built for everyday sums such as a bill, the shopping, change or a discount. It works like a desk calculator: it shows the whole sum, calculates from left to right and handles percentages the way a shop till does.

Does Ivory Calculator need the internet?

No. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet at all. It works fully offline, including in airplane mode.

Does Ivory Calculator have ads, an account or tracking?

No. There are no ads, no account or sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. The app asks for no permissions and collects no data. Nothing leaves your phone.

Is Ivory Calculator a good calculator for seniors and older adults?

Yes. It was designed with seniors and older adults in mind, and it is just as easy for everyone else. The keys are large and well spaced, the text is clear and follows your phone’s font size setting, the whole sum stays on screen so it can be checked, and an optional Speak button reads the answer aloud. There are only two settings.

Can I set it up on a parent’s or grandparent’s phone?

Yes. There are only two settings, key press sound and read aloud, so setting it up for a parent, grandparent or elderly relative takes a moment. Choose them once and it is ready.

How do percentages work?

They work like a shop calculator: 200 − 10% = 180, 200 + 15% = 230, 200 × 10% = 20 and 200 ÷ 10% = 2,000. The display explains the result, for example ‘10% of 200 = 20’.

In what order does it calculate?

From left to right, like a desk calculator, so 2 + 3 × 4 = 20.

Does it keep a history of my sums?

Yes. Past sums keeps the last 20 calculations like a till receipt, and you can tap one to use its answer again. It is kept on your phone only and can be cleared at any time, after a confirmation.

How accurate is it?

It uses exact decimal arithmetic, so 0.1 + 0.2 is exactly 0.3. Answers have at most 15 digits and 12 decimals.

How much does Ivory Calculator cost?

The price is 2.99 US dollars, paid once. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising. Local prices and taxes vary by country.

Which Android versions and devices does it support?

Android 8.0 and newer, on phones and tablets. The download is about 1 MB and the app starts quickly, so it suits older phones too.

Is Ivory Calculator available yet?

It is being prepared for release on Google Play. This page will link to the store listing as soon as it is live.
